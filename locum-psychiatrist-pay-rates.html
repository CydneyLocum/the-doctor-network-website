Common questions

Locum psychiatry pay, explained.

How much does a locum psychiatrist earn per day in Australia?

Based on the consultant locum roles currently on our board, the median quoted rate is $2,500 per day. Rates vary with location, setting, on-call, urgency and whether travel and accommodation are included.

Why do locum psychiatry rates vary so much?

Remote and regional roles, short-notice cover, heavy on-call and hard-to-fill sub-specialties usually pay more. Metro roles and longer bookings tend to sit lower, but are often easier to fit around life.

Are travel and accommodation included?

For most regional and remote locums, yes: flights, accommodation and often a car are covered on top of the day rate. Check each role, as packages differ.

Do registrars and CMOs earn the same?

No. These figures are for consultant roles only. Registrar, trainee and career medical officer locums are paid at different rates.
