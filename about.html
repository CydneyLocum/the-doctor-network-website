About

Built from the parts of recruitment that needed fixing.

The Doctor Network brings together more than a decade of recruitment experience and software developed to solve recurring problems around systems, process and speed to market.

Recruitment is fundamentally about relationships.

Across multiple agencies, the human side was rarely the problem. The recurring challenge was the infrastructure around it: disconnected systems, duplicated admin, information in different places and opportunities moving too slowly.

Over time, software was developed to bridge that gap. The Doctor Network brings those two sides together — recruitment experience and purpose-built technology — so more time goes into better conversations.
