Angiography job · WA

Radiographer – Angiography

Angiography suite, private hospital. Locum with potential to go permanent. Travel and accommodation provided. $500 welcome bonus.

LocumPrivateRadiographyAngiography
  • Location: Perth, WA
  • Dates: ASAP – Ongoing
  • Rate / salary: $85 per hour (negotiable)

Overview

Locum radiographer for a busy private hospital angiography suite in Perth, covering coronary and structural heart, electrophysiology studies, vascular and interventional radiology, plus some general radiology. A dedicated radiographer educator supports your onboarding, and the team of nurses and radiographers works closely together. Travel and accommodation are provided, there is potential to go permanent, and a $500 welcome bonus is paid once you have completed 10 shifts. Requirements: 2+ years angiography experience (cardiac, vascular or IR), AHPRA registration, radiography qualifications, a current radiation licence and Australian citizenship, PR or unrestricted work rights. CT experience desirable. A police check from the last 12 months is required; a Working with Children Check and immunisation evidence may be needed.

Role details

Full-time. Locum with potential to go permanent.
