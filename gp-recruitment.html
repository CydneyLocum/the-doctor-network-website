Common questions

Good to know.

Can you find a GP locum at short notice?

Often, yes, depending on location, dates and the billing arrangements on offer. Let us know the dates, the practice's location and Modified Monash or DPA status, and whether travel and accommodation are covered.

What is a Distribution Priority Area (DPA)?

A DPA is a location the Australian Government classifies as having a shortage of GP services. Many overseas-trained doctors, and some other doctors with Medicare provider number restrictions, need to work in a DPA to bill Medicare. We can help match doctors with DPA roles.

What does a GP need to work as a locum?

General or specialist registration with AHPRA, a Medicare provider number for the location, professional indemnity insurance, and either Fellowship (FRACGP or FACRRM) or an approved pathway, plus the practice's own credentialing requirements.

Do you recruit permanent GPs as well as locums?

Yes. We help practices with permanent GP appointments as well as locum and float cover.
